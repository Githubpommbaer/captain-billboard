FAQ

Questions you were going to ask anyway.

Is this an endorsement?

No. He rents 25×25 cm, chest and back. He does not love your product. He will not say he loves your product. If you need a love letter, hire someone who writes love letters. This is a billboard with knees.

What do I actually get?

Paid space on chest and/or back in the clips. You send a logo. We make the files. Stills on The drop, stills and a short clip from Field trip up. The season is a month of that, not a spiritual partnership.

How big is the space?

25×25 cm on the chest. Same again on the back. Two squares. Four gold arrows already doing the pointing, so your designer can relax.

How fast can this happen?

We make the clips after we have the logo. If your brand team needs twelve people to approve a comma, start the comma now. We will not invent a day-count so this looks like a courier.

Who is Dave?

An AI-made superhero. Not a person. Nobody puts on the suit. Nobody walks a street. Blank placard, human invoice address. Faceless on purpose. The square is the talent. The walk cycle is a file.

Can the art say Dave loves this?

No. See house rule 07. Also see the entire personality of this page. I’m not being cute. I’m being consistent.

Can I pick the location?

Yes, as a setting in the clip, within the booking and the house rules. Rooftops, streets, transit, a laundry that will never recover — talk to us. We do not do active runways. Nobody dies for a yoghurt. Nobody even leaves the hard drive.

Who can use the pictures?

You can use the delivered files for the booked campaign. We keep the right to show the work as “a man wore a square.” Fine print goes in the booking note, not in a surprise tweet.

Can I put a QR code on it?

If it fits, scans, and isn’t a scam. A QR to a 40-page PDF is how you waste 25×25 cm. A QR to one clear page is how you pretend you’re modern.

Is this a joke?

Yes. Also no. Also there is a rate card and an email address. The joke is the honesty. The invoice is real. Both things can sit on the same chest.
