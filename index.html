FAQ

Questions you were going to ask anyway.

Is this an endorsement?

No. He rents 40×25 cm. He does not love your product. He will not say he loves your product. If you need a love letter, hire someone who writes love letters. This is a billboard with knees.

What do I actually get?

Paid space on chest and/or back for the booked dates, printed from your art, plus field documentation — stills on the walk-on, stills and a short clip from Field trip up. The season is a month of that, not a spiritual partnership.

How big is the space?

40×25 cm on the chest. Same again on the back. Two rectangles. Four gold arrows already doing the pointing, so your designer can relax.

How fast can this happen?

Five days from approved artwork to first field files. If your brand team needs twelve people to approve a comma, start the comma now.

Who is Dave?

An AI-made superhero with a blank placard and a very human invoice address. Faceless on purpose. The rectangle is the talent. The man is the walk cycle.

Can the art say Dave loves this?

No. See house rule 07. Also see the entire personality of this page. I’m not being cute. I’m being consistent.

Can I pick the location?

Yes, within the booking, the city, and the house rules. Rooftops, streets, transit, a laundry that will never recover — talk to us. We do not do active runways or other ways to die for a yoghurt.

Who can use the pictures?

You can use the delivered files for the booked campaign. We keep the right to show the work as “a man wore a rectangle.” Fine print goes in the booking note, not in a surprise tweet.

Can I put a QR code on it?

If it fits, scans, and isn’t a scam. A QR to a 40-page PDF is how you waste 40×25 cm. A QR to one clear page is how you pretend you’re modern.

Is this a joke?

Yes. Also no. Also there is a rate card and an email address. The joke is the honesty. The invoice is real. Both things can sit on the same chest.
